# BEST KOTA Website

**Big Taste. Real Satisfaction.**

A complete, modern, mobile-friendly website for BEST KOTA – your favourite kota spot in Soshanguve, Pretoria.

## Files Included

- `index.html` – Main website (all pages/sections in one)
- `styles.css` – Beautiful styling (gold, black & white theme)
- `script.js` – Open/Closed status, mobile menu, contact form → WhatsApp

## Features

✅ Sticky header with mobile hamburger menu  
✅ Live Open / Closed banner (based on South Africa time 07:30–18:00)  
✅ Hero section with strong call-to-action  
✅ Why Choose Us features  
✅ Full Menu (Classic R45, Cheese R55, Russian R60, Super R75)  
✅ One-click WhatsApp ordering with pre-filled messages  
✅ How to Order steps  
✅ Special Offers section  
✅ About Us + Our Promise  
✅ Food Gallery (placeholders – replace with real photos)  
✅ Customer Reviews (example reviews – replace with real ones)  
✅ Location + Google Maps embed  
✅ Contact page with form (sends to WhatsApp)  
✅ Floating WhatsApp button  
✅ Fully responsive (looks great on phones)  
✅ Gold / Black / White brand colours  

## How to Use

1. Open `index.html` in any browser to preview.
2. Upload the three files (`index.html`, `styles.css`, `script.js`) to any web host (Netlify, GitHub Pages, Hostinger, etc.).
3. Replace the gallery placeholders with real photos of your kotas.
4. Replace the example reviews with real customer reviews.
5. Add your real Facebook, Instagram and TikTok links in the footer once the accounts are created.
